Storefront

A game's own art and a short brief in. Store icon, stills, banner and listing copy out.

storefront · Page · Contract

One reading of the game's reference art becomes a direction every surface inherits. Each surface is drawn from its own brief, cut to its exact store canvas, checked and reviewed, and the listing copy is written beside them.

Steps

StepNode types
Read the request
The storefront document, its positioning note and the game's own reference art are read and bound by digest.
Read the storefront request storefront/storefront.resolve
Direction and copy
One reading of the references is compiled into a direction every surface inherits, and the listing copy is written beside it.
Compile the art direction storefront/direction.compile
Write the store listing storefront/listing.compile
Draw each surface
Each surface is drawn from its own brief against the references, cut to its exact canvas, and refused if its size, alpha or bytes are wrong.
Draw the surface storefront/surface.generate
Fit the shipping canvas storefront/surface.normalize
Admit the surface storefront/surface.validate
Review
A reviewer that did not draw the surface judges a smaller copy, and the verdict is recorded with the picture.
Make a review copy storefront/surface.proxy
Review the surface storefront/surface.review
Record the surface storefront/surface.record
Close
The surfaces, the copy and the references are closed into one package.
Close the storefront package storefront/storefront.close

Outputs

package/surfaces/app_icon.pngEach surface at the exact canvas its store asks for
package/listing.jsonThe listing copy
package/inventory.jsonEvery surface and reference in the closed package
