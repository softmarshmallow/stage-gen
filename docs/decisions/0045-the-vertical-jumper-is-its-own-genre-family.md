# 0045 — The vertical jumper is its own genre family, not a camera mode in the runner

*Ruled while reserving future genres.*

## Fact

The temptation is to add a ratcheting-camera name to the runner's camera vocabulary and call
the vertical endless jumper done.

## Challenge

The runner already has authored rectangular occupancy, difficulty ranks, ceiling selection,
props, items and a camera that admits a vertical follow axis. Almost everything transfers.

## Ruling

It is its own genre member family. Three of the runner track contract's load-bearing
invariants are horizontal *by construction* rather than by parameter, and a mode flag makes
each conditionally meaningless: the walk surface row is the seam datum the whole validator is
built around and a jumper has no continuous contact surface at all; the bottom contiguous
surface row is a bottom-up column scan every hazard, pickup and pit-run check calls; and the
maximum clear gap measures a quantity a bounce does not have. One contract with two mutually
exclusive readings selected by a mode flag is exactly the compatibility-reader shape this
repository forbids. The verb set inverts too: the runner's one verb is an edge-triggered jump,
the jumper's is a held continuous steering axis with no jump at all, which the intent latch's
consume-on-sample semantics actively corrupts. Build the Doodle Jump variant, not Icy Tower:
auto-bounce plus screen wrap collapses reachability to a single one-dimensional condition on
delta-y, whereas landing-momentum jump height makes reachability a function of prior traversal
and is not provable ahead of time. The seam rule's replacement is a **landing band** — each
band declares an entry and an exit datum row, its lowest platform sits within the rise limit of
the entry datum, and its topmost platform *is* the exit datum.

## Evidence

The precedent is the runner itself, which reused the shared side-view modules rather than
extending the platformer. What transfers is substantial and what does not is specific: the
seamless axis is horizontal in the contract and the loop construction module is column-native
end to end, so the reflection tier transposes for free while the interior and anchored tiers
need their conditioning masks re-derived on rows. A y-loop asset module is the one genuine
asset-side blocker, and the asset taxonomy already reserved it and already says the demo is
impossible until it has a caller. The avatar art does not transfer either: a run strip is wrong
for a game whose avatar never runs, and one jump strip cannot serve both halves of a bounce.

## Falsifier

A formulation of the three invariants that is genuinely axis-parametric rather than
conditionally meaningless — which would make one contract with a parameter honest.
