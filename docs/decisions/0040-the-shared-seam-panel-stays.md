# 0040 — The shared seam panel stays; what it carries is art direction

*Ruled 2026-09-03.*

## Fact

Every chunk carries the same shared two-column bridge at both edges, so any join is continuous
by construction. The cost the contract could not see is that whatever those two columns
contain is republished at every junction in the finished track, seconds apart — in the shipped
run, a coral pipe run under a hanging vine.

## Challenge

The recorded plan was to replace the shared panel with edge *conditioning*, so each chunk
paints its own aprons.

## Ruling

That plan was wrong and the shared panel stays. Under the seam rule any chunk may follow any
chunk, so pixel-exact continuity needs both sides of a cut to carry bytes derived from shared
inputs: two independently painted aprons differ in tone at the cut, which is a visible edge
rather than none; per-pair bridges are quadratic; runtime cross-fading blends two pictures into
mush. What changes is what the panel *carries*. The aprons are asked for as the bolted joint
between two spans — the walking-surface band running level and unbroken through them and no
pipe, conduit, cable, vine, leaf, flower, hatch, lit fitting or lettering. How quiet a joint
should be is art direction, so the incident share is *recorded* rather than refused: this
repository puts art direction in the prompt.

## Evidence

Every published tile's first and last column were byte identical, and each painting's own
aprons carried 0.71 to 1.29 times the colour incident of the span they joined; regenerated
under the joint clause they carry 0.48 to 0.81, and a join reads as a plain bolted plate. Edge
response was tried as the measure first and cannot see this at all — a plain bolted plate has
as much contour as a pipe behind a vine. The first phrasing of the joint clause was long enough
to crowd out the demand that every guide cell be painted, and two segments started failing the
coverage checks under it; stating the top edge on its own line fixed both.

## Falsifier

A join that reads as a repeat rather than as infrastructure at the shipped chunk width — which
would move the cost from the panel's content to the panel itself.
