# ElevenLabs v3 anime voices

A voice is listed here only after a person has listened to it on
[Eleven v3](../spec/model-eleven-v3.md) and accepted it for anime-style game
characters. Library metadata, names and descriptions are not a listening
verdict; they only choose what gets auditioned.

Reference a voice by its ID. Names are not unique in the Voice Library: on
2026-09-14 a search returned 23 voices named "Annie", 23 "Aki" and 21 "Yuki".

## What the audition found

- The account's premade voices are general narration voices. Cast as game
  characters they read like a documentary or video voice-over.
- Native-Japanese anime voices fit the characters better than native-Korean
  narration voices, including when they read Korean.
- A library voice can be used straight from the text-to-speech route, but the
  six above were added to the account so they are listed with it.

## How a voice is auditioned

Every candidate reads the same short line at stability 0, with v3 audio tags
covering a calm half and an emotional half, so the comparison is between
voices, not scripts. The 2026-09-14 Korean round covered 108 voices: the 106
standard-rate native-Korean female library voices plus two already in the
account. It billed 4,648 characters. Its clips and listening page stay local,
under Afterlight's ignored `art/voice-audition-ko/`.

The Korean line:

> [softly] 괜찮아, 여기선 아무도 널 해치지 않아. …잠깐. [gasps] 저 불빛, 방금 움직였어! [urgent] 다들 뒤로 물러서!
