Giving a finished sprite a blink and a few mouth shapes by hand means cutting out the face, drawing
each state to match the original line work, lining the drawings up, masking them, and checking that
nothing else on the sprite moved. Stage Gen holds that as one graph. A vision model finds the face; an
image model draws every state in one sheet so they match each other; each drawing is aligned to the
original and outlined; a reviewer that did not draw them judges every combination; and the accepted
patches go back on the full sprite with every other pixel left exactly as it was. What comes out is
not a fixed animation: each eye and the mouth is its own patch at one fixed offset, so a game can
blink, wink and talk independently, in any order and at any pace.

## Where it works

<Scope>
  <Works>One finished character image, with or without transparency</Works>
  <Works>Faces with clearly drawn open eyes and a readable mouth</Works>
  <Works>Full-body sprites: the face is found and cropped automatically</Works>
  <Works>Faces large enough that their line work reads at the sprite's own size</Works>
  <Limit>Full lip sync: this run has two mouth shapes, A and O, not a complete set of speech shapes</Limit>
  <Limit>Faces too small to read: enlarging them does not add detail</Limit>
  <Limit>Eyes with hair drawn across them: the outline may be refused</Limit>
  <Limit>Head or body movement: only the eyes and mouth change</Limit>
  <Tip>A result can be partial. If the mouth is refused but the eyes pass, the original mouth stays and the blink still works.</Tip>
  <Tip>The eyes were reviewed as a pair. Winking one eye composes exactly, but was not judged on its own.</Tip>
  <Tip>It reviews still pictures. Watch the animation play before accepting it.</Tip>
  <Tip>Pairs with Movie sprite: use the loop's first frame as the source, and the face states sit on the moving body.</Tip>
</Scope>

## What goes wrong by hand

<Guards>
  <Guard node="admission" how="A reviewer decides feature by feature what can change. Anything unsure keeps its original drawing.">Time goes into an eye hidden by hair, or a mouth that cannot be redrawn.</Guard>
  <Guard node="registration" how="Each drawing is aligned to the original and refused if it needs more than a small shift, turn or change of scale.">A new drawing comes back slightly moved, and the face swims when it blinks.</Guard>
  <Guard node="geometry" how="The outline is refused when it would cut through hair in front of the eye.">An eye mask cuts into the hair that sits over it.</Guard>
  <Guard node="quality" how="A reviewer that did not draw the states checks every combination for changes around the eyes and mouth.">A blink also changes the hair beside the eye.</Guard>
  <Guard node="face_reconstruction" how="Every pixel outside the patches, and all of the transparency, is checked identical to the original.">Pixels outside the face change, or the sprite's edges shift.</Guard>
</Guards>
