# Iron Petal Unit

The orbital greenhouse Halcyon Arbor keeps a chain of small settlements alive by growing food,
restoring seed stock, and repairing damaged habitat modules. Its oldest maintenance decks have
become a bright tangle of glass, irrigation machinery, roots, and service rails.

Eleven-year-old mechanic-pilot Mira Vale answers emergency beacons from the open saddle of Petal
Unit Seven, a compact coral-and-cream rescue machine. Rider and machine move as one runner: Mira
steers and repairs while the unit jumps broken service lanes, slides beneath jammed irrigation
arches, and gathers luminous seed cells needed to restart the greenhouse's great life-support
flower.

The tone is optimistic rescue adventure. The greenhouse is endangered but inhabited, maintained,
and worth saving; obstacles are failed equipment and vigorous plant growth rather than enemies.
All names, devices, plants, and imagery are original and brand-neutral.
