# The Grain — true chronology

Status: **derived from the finished draft (Scenes 1–52), 2026-09-02**. This is what
actually happened, not what anyone said happened; the accounts are in
`evidence-ledger.md`. Where the script gives a clock time it is quoted; where a
time is inferred, it is marked *(inferred)*. Chapter references are Ch/Scene.

## Working calendar

The script never prints a date. The internal evidence fixes it: the supper is a
Wednesday; the Ellery file runs MAY to SEPTEMBER; Havel has wanted to meet R.E.
"for four months"; Nell turned twenty-nine "last month"; on the Sunday drive the
Wilshire windows show "mannequins in fur stand in October"; the ledger is stamped
1958 and June died "fourteen years ago." The only Wednesday that satisfies all of
it is **Wednesday 27 September 1972**. Nothing in the text depends on the exact
date; this calendar exists so that "last week," "in May," "eleven weeks," and
"three weeks ago" can be checked against one ruler.

## Backstory

- **c. 1942–44.** Robert Holt meets Lydia Shaw ("thirty years," Marian; "twenty-eight," Robert). He knew of her before he met Marian. Lydia lends the Holts thirty dollars for curtains in the Burbank room with the painted window.
- **1943.** Nell Avery born (twenty-nine in August 1972). Lydia gives June the green wool blanket.
- **1946.** Lydia joins Tolland's (twenty-six years by 1972).
- **1958, winter.** *Private Weather*, Arthur Rusk directing. The set stands eleven days, nine photographed. June Avery, forty-one, performs beside the wardrobe, unpaid except for the days she performed. Lydia keeps continuity and wardrobe; Marian holds June's coat every day. Rehearsals happen in June's home and are recorded on six small reels; June's line "Don't improve it just because it's over" is said in rehearsal and put into the next morning's pages by Rusk, under no writer's name. June also says, after a take, "Coming indoors isn't coming back. I only said he could come in."
- **March 1958.** The picture stops. Lydia numbers the six reels in green ink at June's kitchen table. The reels and a blue folder of dialogue pages are marked RETURNED TO JUNE on Lydia's index card.
- **December 1958.** June dies of her heart in a hospital; Marian is with her; Lydia is at the store; Robert drives Nell from school and is too late. Nell is fifteen. Marian and Lydia pack the reels and folder into a box marked JUNE — WORK (Marian's hand). Marian asks Lydia to get rid of the rest; Lydia keeps the ledger, contact sheets, stills, and carbons in the Tolland's alterations workroom for fourteen years.
- **1958–1969.** JUNE — WORK sits in the Holts' garage for eleven years.
- **c. 1965–66.** Ruth and Henry meet on "the old case"; Ruth tells him a truth that costs her. Six years before the supper. Frances, from that case, still sends Ruth a Christmas card to the old address. Lydia has had Ruth's address for six years (Ruth says seven).
- **c. 1969–70.** Nell and Paul move in together; Lydia gives Nell the blanket; Nell takes every box from the garage, JUNE — WORK included, into the hall closet.
- **1968.** The Winter Room stops making orange ice.
- **June 1972** (Lydia: "You cut it." "In June.") is Nell's haircut; not to be confused with June the person.

## The four months (May–September 1972)

- **May.** Paul leaves Nell with his clothes, "two boxes of papers," and the good frying pan. One box is JUNE — WORK, relabeled PAUL — PAPERS over Marian's label. He arrives at Ruth's apartment after midnight and sleeps on her couch six nights. At her kitchen table Ruth gives him the sentence he says to Nell: "We had already left each other. I was only the first to go." Ruth pays the deposit on Room 26 at the Kenton Rooms, South Alvarado; Paul gives her the address and, after locking himself out twice, a spare room key, which she drops in the back of her desk drawer.
- **May.** Paul opens production account 418-R with Tremont Annex (Rusk's *A Single Light*, Miriam Havel associate producer). His source declaration reads SOURCE AUTHORITY: THROUGH FAMILY; RIGHTS CONTACT: P. ELLERY; Havel writes VERIFICATION PENDING. He delivers June's reels and folder in two lots (invoice carbon: SECOND DELIVERY); Havel makes working transfers and photostats. Paul begins bringing Ruth untitled pages for dialogue passes; the vouchers are OUTSIDE DIALOGUE — R.E., payable to Paul; he pays Ruth less than he is paid. He tells Havel that R.E. requires anonymity.
- **After Paul leaves (May/June).** Lydia visits Nell, sees the empty shelf, and knows what the two boxes held. She does not tell Nell.
- **June.** Paul telephones the Holts' house; Robert answers, refuses Nell's address, takes his number, tells Lydia, does not tell Marian.
- **Third assignment.** Ruth asks the title; Paul says it will change.
- **Early July (eleven weeks before the supper).** The original reels and folder go back to Paul from Bungalow Six.
- **Fifth assignment.** A cough and a false start in the margin; Ruth types PRIVATE RECORDING? at the foot of her cover. Paul answers in pencil on the original, REHEARSAL. FAMILY CLEARED., returns it with the sixth assignment; Ruth writes P.E.: REHEARSAL. FAMILY CLEARED. on the fifth account card and initials nothing further. The sixth assignment is dated nine days after the fifth. Ruth does not ask again.
- **Around 6 September (three weeks before).** Havel telephones Lydia at Tolland's giving only the initials M.H. and the code 418-R, asking about the bedroom, the wardrobe, and June's line. Lydia writes the pink message slip and leaves the return number blank. Marian, told, asks Lydia a second time to destroy the box.
- **Week of 18 September.** Robert takes Lydia's list to the Kenton Rooms (first visit). Tuesday 26 September: Robert moves Lydia's bookcase, packs the coffee pot in carton six, and moves the deep wardrobe carton from the alterations workroom to Westlake under a transfer tag Edwin wrote: DISCARDED PATTERNS over PRIVATE PROPERTY, E. PRICE in the authorization box. Edwin does not ask what is inside.
- **Wednesday afternoon (unfixed).** A sack of whiting splits on the gallery of the unfinished window. The dressers leave it and go down the access stair at four.

## Tuesday 3 October and after

- **Tuesday 10:00 a.m.** Bungalow Six. Nell's terms: originals to her; every copy sealed with Marian under June's name, nobody plays it; Ruth's passes are Ruth's and nobody asks her; "Then shoot something else in it... Have her answer first." Havel writes "permission."
