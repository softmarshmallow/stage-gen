# Bellweather

Bellweather is a bright, oversized fantasy valley where village bells keep the
stories of roads, creatures, and people from being forgotten. When pages begin
blowing out of the sealed royal chronicle, familiar local creatures become
mischievous story-beasts and paths rearrange themselves around missing
chapters.

The Wayfarer is a young-adult novice adventurer from Sunpetal Crossing. They
leave the village with a wooden training sword, a travel satchel, and no chosen
class, recover scattered pages, help residents, grow into a specialized role,
and follow Crowncrag Road through Amberbell Forest to the distant castle.

The core fantasy is to be a tiny, expressive hero in a world that feels
enormous, welcoming, collectible-rich, and full of visible destinations. Its
rhythm is village conversation and preparation, side-scrolling travel,
playful combat, loot and discovery, then a warm return home.

## Visual identity

- Clean, high-resolution 2D digital game art.
- Characters are approximately 2.25 heads tall, with large expressive heads
  and eyes, compact torsos, simplified hands and feet, and crisp silhouettes.
- Actors and interactive creatures use controlled dark contours, saturated
  fills, and two-to-three-band cel shading.
- Environments use clean digital gouache-like color blocking, rounded irregular
  silhouettes, decorative curls, and texture confined to individual stone,
  wood, leaf, cloth, water, and cloud surfaces.
- Side-view scenes separate dark playable footing, a saturated interactive
  middle plane, and progressively softer atmospheric background planes.
- Sky blue, moss green, turquoise, warm brass, cream, peach, coral, and
  lavender carry a cheerful, adventurous, sociable mood.
