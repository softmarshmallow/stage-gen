## Numbers, times, dates

- Dialogue: clock times in words (네 시 십 분, 여덟 시 십 분, 여섯 시). Counts in words up to
  twenty (열일곱 장), numerals above that (스물여섯 해 → 26년 is acceptable in dialogue when
  the English uses a numeral-like precision; prefer words).
- Action lines: numerals for clock times (오후 4시 10분) and for dates (9월 27일 수요일).
- Years stay numerals (1958년, 1972년).
- Weekdays: 수요일 목요일 금요일 토요일 일요일 월요일 화요일.
- Money, page numbers, room numbers, catalogue numbers (418-R): unchanged.

## Punctuation and typography

- Quotations inside dialogue and action: "…" (straight or curly double quotes). Film
  titles: 《…》. Emphasis: keep *asterisks* around the emphasized Korean word.
- Ellipsis: 한국어 줄임표 …… may be used where the English uses "..."; the English rarely does.
- Em dashes in the English ("Seventeen loose sheets—") become a Korean dash — or a comma;
  do not add dashes the English does not have.
- Typed, handwritten, and signage inserts (`!` lines) keep the English verbatim, followed by
  a Korean gloss in parentheses on the same line: `!4:10 — P.E. / COVER 5 (4시 10분 — P.E. / 표지 5)`.
