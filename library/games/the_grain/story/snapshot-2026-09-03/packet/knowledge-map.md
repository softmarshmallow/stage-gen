# The Grain — knowledge map

Status: **derived from the finished draft, 2026-09-02**. For each principal: what
they know at the supper, what they believe wrongly, what they hide, and when each
item reaches the page. "Learns" rows are for Henry (and the reader), who knows
nothing at the start.

## Ruth Ellery

**Knows Wednesday before supper.** Paul left Nell in May with her sentence in his mouth; where he sleeps; that she has been paid through Paul for six untitled dialogue passes on account 418-R; that the fifth packet read like a private recording; that Paul answered "REHEARSAL. FAMILY CLEARED." and she did not ask again; that Paul collected that cover at 4:10 today and was pleased with himself; that Lydia intends to forgive her for something Lydia has not named.
**Does not know.** That Paul was invited; that the material is June Avery's; the title *Private Weather*; that Havel exists by name.
**Learns.** Paul's presence (Ch2); the burned-chicken sentence is June's and Paul knows why (Ch3); Nell's condition and Paul's 5:45 call (Ch7); the ledger, *Private Weather*, Rusk (Ch9, told by Nell); Havel and the vouchers (Ch10); that Paul's declaration named her, and how (Ch14).
**Hides, and when it comes out.** The door (Ch12); the pages (Ch13); the wrist (Ch14); the push, the button, the pages chosen (Ch15). Her sentence for Paul in May (Ch7, volunteered under Nell's pressure). The checks (Ch9, when the invoice is found). R.E. (Ch10, when the voucher initials are read). The fifth cover's absence and Paul's 4:10 visit (Ch11, when the pocket is empty).
**Lies to.** Ward and Henry (Wednesday, Friday); Henry alone (Sunday). Not to Nell about the pages; she says "I don't know" (Ch12).

## Henry Calder

**Knows.** Ruth from the old case, six years; that she prefers to be heard rather than found out.
**Learns, in order.** The dinner's purpose and cast (Ch1–2); Paul (Ch2); the sentence, the envelope, the carton, the key (Ch3); the shoe, the coffee, "not to wait" (Ch4); the body, the corner's heading, the marks, the button, the carton, the whiting (Ch5); Ruth's Wednesday account (Ch6); the 5:45 call, Nell's lie, Paul's room, Ruth's sentence for Paul (Ch7); the ledger, June, Rusk, 418-R, M.H., the transfer tag, Robert's part (Ch8); JUNE — WORK, the sleeves, Ruth's checks, Havel's name and appointment, the two eggs, "Help me / I said no" (Ch9); *A Single Light*, THROUGH FAMILY, R.E., the three packets, the workprint, the tape, the corrected declaration's purpose (Ch10); the black file, the fifth cover, the account card, 4:10 (Ch11); the timings, the hatch, the whiting's origin, the lip, one pace, the bells, the carton inventory, Bell's three layers, the two sets of tracks, the Friday account (Ch12); the folder identified, the overlays, the bundle, the tear (Ch13); the declaration's text, the third account (Ch14).
**Withholds.** The car, from Thursday evening to Monday 7 a.m. (Ch14: "I am giving myself one [night]"). The shoe, from Wednesday to Friday, and then cannot say it to her face (Ch15).
**Finds.** The first-leg contradiction (vestibule vs. lip) and the second (wrist vs. no). "This time I found it."

## Nell Avery

**Knows.** Paul called at 5:45 and she set the condition; Paul took two boxes; the frying pan; Ruth gave Paul his leaving words (she "remembers the words").
**Does not know.** What was in the boxes; that June performed and wrote for *Private Weather*; that Lydia kept an archive; that Marian asked for it destroyed; that Robert met Paul.
**Learns.** Paul is dead (Ch6); the carton was for her (Ch6); Ruth's exact sentence and its authorship (Ch7); the ledger, the contact sheet, June's hand, June's sentence, the slip, the index card, Marian's two requests, Robert's calls and visits, the transfer (Ch8); JUNE — WORK, Ruth's checks (Ch9); Havel, the declaration, R.E., the packets, June's voice (Ch10); the fifth cover (Ch11); the lip, the button, the tracks, Ruth's Friday account (Ch12); the folder in the bag; the overlays (told badly, offstage between Ch13 and Ch14); the declaration's text (Ch14); the push (Ch16).
**Hides.** The call, one night. Nothing after Thursday morning: "Nothing of mine has had to be found."

## Lydia Shaw

**Knows.** Everything about 1958; that the reels and folder went to June and then to Nell's hall closet; that Paul took them (from the empty shelf, May); Paul's number (from Robert, June); that Havel's office called about the bedroom (three weeks ago); that Marian wants the box destroyed; that Edwin wrote the tag; that Paul agreed to bring the material back tonight; that Paul accepted at six; that Ruth wrote Paul's leaving words (she intends to forgive her for it at the table).
**Does not know.** That Paul sold copies; the dialogue passes; R.E.; that Paul asked Ruth to tell Nell for him; anything about the gallery.
**Hides, and when.** Paul's invitation from Ruth and Nell (Ch2); the box's existence from Nell for fourteen years (Ch6 Sc14, Ch8); her method ("I am not proud... not ashamed") (Ch8 Sc21).
**Speech pattern.** Speeches begun and cut off (Ch6 Sc14, Ch8 Sc21, Ch13 Sc39, Ch14 Sc44).

## Marian Holt

**Knows.** 1958 from the coat's point of view; that June "spoke before she knew which parts men would call the picture"; that she asked Lydia twice to destroy the record; that JUNE — WORK was in her garage eleven years.
**Does not know until the page.** That Robert answered Paul's June call, visited him twice, and moved the archive (Ch8 Sc21); that Robert threatened to tell Nell himself (Ch14).
**Hides.** Nothing about the crime. She keeps her second request to Lydia until Lydia says "And three weeks ago" (Ch8 Sc20).
**Corrects.** Everyone; fails to find anything to correct once (Ch14 Sc44).

## Robert Holt

**Knows.** Paul's June call and number; that he gave it to Lydia; both Kenton visits; the sandwich; the blue folder on the bed; the transfer from alterations; that he told Paul he would tell Nell where the box was if Paul did not bring it.
**Hides.** All of it from Marian, until Ch8; the threat until Paul's declaration says it (Ch14).
**Never lies about.** Soup. Two pages of it in his statement (Ch14 Sc42).

## Edwin Price

**Knows.** That Lydia has asked him four things in twenty-six years and this was the fourth; that he wrote DISCARDED PATTERNS without asking what the carton held; that he gave Paul the key; the building's doors, bolts, and lifts.
**Hides.** Nothing once asked. Statement Thursday (Ch12 Sc36). Cannot finish his one thing (Ch14 Sc44).

## Bell

**Knows.** Exactly what he did: the carton on the counter; the key from Paul's right hand to Ruth's jacket; four minutes in receiving; fourteen minutes of windows; the stage door opened far enough to see a face. What was in the carton, three layers, "That's the only part I wrote."
**Learns.** That the button rang in his ear and would have been heard over the telephone (Ch12); that there was no bell to hear (Ch14).
**Hides.** Nothing. Repeats.

## Paul Ellery (dead from Ch5)

**Knew.** That the boxes were June's; that he sold them under THROUGH FAMILY; that Ruth asked once and he answered so she would not ask again; that Nell would listen if he spoke first; that Robert would tell Nell if he did not bring the box; that he was correcting "more than one account."
**Told.** Nell: "something that should have reached you years ago" (5:45). Lydia: yes (6:00). Ruth: "Not if I get there tomorrow" (4:10). Bell: "That's the only part I wrote." Edwin: the carton is downstairs. Ruth in the vestibule: tell Nell for me. Ruth on the gallery: "I'm putting it all in. Even this. Help me say it so she can stand to hear it." "You owe her that." "Ruth." "Help me."
**Wrote.** The letter to Nell (police hold it; Ward reads it to her Friday; it admits the box and promises the rest in person, naming no one). The corrected declaration, seventeen loose sheets revising a tied draft, with the fifth cover attached: Lydia, Robert, Edwin, Nell, Ruth, each "what I told each person, and what was true."

## Ellis Ward

**Knows by the end of Wednesday.** The lift pair; Ruth returned alone; the corner; the marks; the button (ordered traced); Henry's hand in the door and his "I don't know."
**By Friday.** The button's circuit; the whiting's origin; the carton's contents; Bell's three layers; the two sets of tracks; the shoe (from Henry); "pleased with himself"; Ruth's Friday account.
**By Saturday.** The overlays, the bundle, the tear direction; Ruth's Saturday account; releases her at ten.
**Monday.** The car and the third account (from Henry at 7 a.m.); the final account; the arrest. He never opens a notebook after Wednesday.
**One joke per chapter.** "Not tonight" (Ch6); "Because there is nowhere clean to put it" (Ch11); "I am not a tall man" (Ch12); "I've had your coffee" (Ch15); the soup (Ch14).

## Miriam Havel

**Knows.** 418-R end to end: the declaration, the two lots, the transfers, the vouchers, R.E.'s anonymity as Paul described it, the Thursday appointment and its purpose. That she wrote "clearance" knowing the difference.
**Learns.** Ruth is R.E. (Ch10); Nell is the rights holder (Ch10); that Nell will not sell (Ch16).

## Mrs. Vale

**Knows.** Paul's rent (five days late, paid through next month), his water, the fan, the groceries, the grocer question, the 3:14 message, the police inventory.
