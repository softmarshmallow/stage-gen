# Ember Hollow — positioning

What the storefront is selling, in the game's own terms. This note is read by the
direction compiler and by the listing writer, and by nothing else.

Ember Hollow is a survival game about one winter in a small valley. You forage,
you craft what you need from what you find, you keep warm, and you keep going.
The seasons turn on a calendar rather than a timer, and winter is the part the
game is actually about: warmth becomes a vital, the ground goes hard, and the
things you could gather freely in autumn are under snow.

The view is elevated and oblique — you look down at the ground plane from above
and slightly behind, close enough to read a face and far enough to see where the
weather is coming from.

The tone is quiet and unhurried. There is no combat, no timer counting down, and
nobody to rescue. The pressure is entirely the season.

What the pictures should say: a small warm thing in a large cold place, and one
person's work holding it there.
