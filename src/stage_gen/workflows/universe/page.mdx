A universe is something to explore rather than play: typed entities, their relationships, the tensions between them and the questions the source leaves open, with exactly one concept image for each entity. It starts from a poster, a synopsis and an expansion direction, and keeps their roles apart: the poster is visual evidence only, and the synopsis holds the world's facts. The semantic phase proposes the world, and it must pass an independent review before anything is drawn. The gallery phase then gives each admitted entity one concept image, reviewed by a model that did not draw it. The set is judged as a whole: a reader who has not seen the synopsis should be able to explain how the world works, who disagrees, and why.
